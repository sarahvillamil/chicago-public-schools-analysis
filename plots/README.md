## Chicago Public Schools Final Project EDA

This repository contains the process of EDA completed on Chicago Public Schools and Demographic Data.

### PNGs
-   'attendance_by_type_graph.png': scatter plot comparing student attendance, 
college enrollment, and school type. 
-   'avg_college_persistence_by_type_graph.png': scatter plot comparing college persistence, 
college enrollment, and school type. 
-   'avg_sat_by_type_graph.png': bar graph comparing SAT scores and school type. 
-   'college_enrollment_by_income_level.png': bar graph comparing 
college enrollment and income level. 
-   'college_enrollment_by_school_type.png': bar graph comparing 
college enrollment and school type. 
-   'correlation_plot_cps.png': correlation plot of Chicago Public Schools 
and Demographic dataset. 
-   'distribution_college_enroll.png': scatter plot of college enrollment rate.
-   'enrollment_vs_persistence_by_type_graph.png': scatter plot of college enrollment rate, 
college persistence rate, and school type. 
-   'five_year_vs_four_year_by_type_graph.png': scatter plot of five year graduation rate, 
four year graduation rate and school type. 
-   'four_year_vs_enrollment_by_type_graph.png': scatter plot of four year graduation rate, 
college enrollment rate, and school type. 
-   'missingness_overall.png': general missingness of Chicago Public Schools 
and Demographic dataset.
